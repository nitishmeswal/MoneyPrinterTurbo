## 3 · Educative story: "Ask your event anything with MCP"

**Style:** storytelling on warm paper. One Monday morning told in chapters, with the time of day as the chapter marker and the narration as Crimson italic book captions. The three steps are taught inside the story, then recapped.
**Music:** `music-rh-story.wav`, 116 BPM, A minor → C. Felt keys and a clock that ticks on every beat carry the problem, so the energy rises instead of dipping. Claps build into "She asked.", the drop lands on Step 01, and the groove runs through the steps.
**Characters:** Maya (head of events), Dana Whitfield (CEO) and the speakers are fictional.

| Time | Beat | On screen | Text |
