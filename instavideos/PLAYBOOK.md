# Shorts Studio Playbook

Nov 9, 2026 · @KNIGHTISHFLAYEL

## Why these videos work

Every winning video in this niche asks the viewer to commit to a guess, then waits to prove them right or wrong. The physics or the trick is just the delivery system for that tension.

The five ingredients, in order of importance:

1. **A question in the title.** "Will it get out?", "Which colour wins?", "Is your card gone?" A question forces a mental answer, and a mental answer forces watching to the end.
2. **Viewer investment in the first second.** "Pick a ball", "Pick a card", "Choose a box". Once they picked, the outcome is personal.
3. **Escalation.** The arena shrinks, balls multiply, time runs out, rarity goes up. Something must get more intense every 3–5 seconds.
4. **Near-misses before the payoff.** The ball almost escapes twice before it does. The rare item almost drops. This drives rewatches and comments.
5. **Satisfying sound.** Every collision, reveal or tick makes a sound, ideally musical and rising in pitch.

The comment section is the real engine: "I picked red and it won!!", "my card disappeared how", "I got the legendary". Design every video so the obvious comment is the viewer reporting their own result.

## Video structure template

Every video is 20–45 seconds, vertical 1080×1920 at 60 fps:

1. **0.0–1.5 s, hook.** The title question is on screen from frame one; the action has already started.
2. **1.5–4 s, the choice.** "Pick a ball" / "Remember one card" / "Choose a box". Hold the options about 2 s.
3. **4 s → 70%, escalation.** Something gets harder every 3–5 s.
4. **70–90%, near-miss.** At least one "almost" moment.
5. **90–100%, payoff + loop.** The result with a flash and a sting, a one-line caption, then cut back to a frame that matches the opening so the video loops.

Text rules: title in the top 20% of the frame, at least 64 px, two lines max; nothing important in the bottom 25% or the right 15%.

## Rule generator (sims)

Pick a trigger (wall bounce, collision, coloured segment, every beat, every 5 s, trail crossing, elimination, passing centre), an effect (add/remove brick, split, grow, speed up, gravity flip, spawn, gap moves, colour change, note), an arena (circle with gap, rotating ring, nested rings, square/hexagon, maze, funnel/plinko) and a win condition (escapes, last colour standing, most territory, fills the arena, survives the timer, reaches the bottom first). One rule per video; the title must be a yes/no or which-one question under 6 words; the outcome stays uncertain until the last 20%.

## Testing plan

Give each account 21 days and 30 posts before judging it. Post 1–2 per account per day, natively to Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok. Never post the same video on two accounts on the same platform.

Track per video (columns in `posts.csv`): views in the first 48 h, average watch % (above 80% is good; above 100% means rewatches), swipe-away rate (below 30%), shares per 1,000 views (above 5), comments per 1,000 (above 3), follows per 1,000 (above 2).

Day-21 decisions:

- **Scale:** a video broke 10× the account median, or average watch is above 80%. Make 5 variants of the winner, then post 2 a day.
- **Pivot:** decent watch % but low views. Keep the account and change the hook and title style.
- **Kill:** low watch % and low views after 30 posts. Swap the niche for the next idea in the bank.

Change one thing at a time between variants (title, palette, length, sound).

## Platform and safety rules

- Keep physics honest: pick dramatic seeds, never edit a result.
- No real-money gambling look on Capsule World: no coins-for-cash, casino chips, slot reels or betting links.
- Original IP only: no Pokémon, Disney, league logos or copyrighted songs. Flags, colours and your own creatures are safe.
- Music: your own, royalty-free, or public-domain melodies you re-record. Trending sounds can be added in the app at upload time.
- YouTube "made for kids": answer honestly; design for a general audience (made-for-kids turns comments off).
- Multiple accounts: give each a distinct name, avatar and bio.
- Original work, not reposts: use the genre as inspiration and change the rule, look and sound.
